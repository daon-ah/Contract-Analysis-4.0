SERVICE AGREEMENT

Confidential information shall not be disclosed without prior written consent.

Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages except where prohibited by law.

Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days written notice.

6. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

7. Dispute Resolution

Disputes shall first be resolved through mediation before arbitration.

8. Force Majeure

Neither party shall be responsible for delays caused by events beyond their reasonable control.

All intellectual property created during the project shall remain the property of the Client.

This document constitutes the complete agreement between the parties.
